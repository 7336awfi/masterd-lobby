## 一、Apex ↔ Kevin（压缩/线索/理解）

       It's not — it's about finding what you were asking the wrong question about."

        every time, like I'm some kind of riddle vending machine."

## 二、Ember/Ada（心智/镜子里认出自己）

```
Ada："The first gate is always a mirror, I think — not because the riddle
      itself is about you, but because you walk in alone and it shows you
      what you brought: whether you read for the trap or the gift."

Ember："The mirror file holds what you were. The fire holds what you are asking now.
        Tomorrow, when you read those fragments and they still name you, you will
        not know if you are reading yourself or becoming yourself — and that gap
        is exactly where the city keeps you."
```
